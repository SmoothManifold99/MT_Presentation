# Master Thesis Presentation

## Non-linear p-form Gauge Theories and Their Deformations

**Master's Thesis in Physics**
**Università degli Studi di Padova**

This repository contains the **LaTeX/Beamer source and presentation slides** for my Master's thesis, *Non-linear p-form Gauge Theories and Their Deformations*.

### Overview

The thesis investigates non-linear deformations of chiral \(p\)-form gauge theories and their relation to stress-tensor deformations of quantum and classical field theories.

The main focus is on **chiral four-form gauge theories in ten dimensions**, with particular attention to the **PST** and **INZ** formulations and to $T\bar{T}$-like and Root-$T\bar{T}$ deformations.

The presentation discusses:

* deformations of QFTs;
* stress-tensor deformations and $T\bar{T}$-like deformations;
* non-linear electrodynamics;
* chiral $p$-form theories;
* INZ approach;
* perturbative approach for studying stress-tensor deformations of free chiral theories.
